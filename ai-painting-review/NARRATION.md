# Narration script (v4)

One line per press. Times are targets at 145 words per minute, plus 0.6 s per press, the timed pauses at the choice, rating and voting moments, and about 8 s for the opening animation. **535 spoken words; ends at about 4:32**, including about 3 s on References. The presenter window (`P`) shows the same lines with a live ahead/behind timer.

Interactive moments: at *Your choice*, click a painting or press 1–4. At *The label*, rate 1–5 by click or key, before and after the label flips. At *Artist?*, click Yes/No twice. The next screen reflects the answers, and every screen also works with no input.
